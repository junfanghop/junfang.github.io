About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Political Science and Scientific Computing at the University of Michigan.

During the 2026 Autumn Quarter, I am a Big Ten Academic Alliance Traveling Scholar at the University of Chicago.

Research Interests: Political Economy of Technology; Political Communication; International Political Economy; International Business and Nonmarket Strategy; Generative and Agentic AI; Computational Social Science.

My dissertation examines social media platforms that also function as digital marketplaces. This combination of social media and e-commerce, known as social shopping, lets users buy products directly through the content they view, such as videos and posts. I study how content creators use political content to attract attention and sell products, and how governments regulate these marketplaces across countries.

My work on China's platforms is part of a broader research agenda on Chinese domestic politics and international relations. I also study AI's political and social implications and the reliability of AI-assisted social science research.
